What this number does and does not mean

The measurement that actually tests the strategy is not the P&L, it is the t-statistic — currently +0.79 on the maker book against a bar of 3.0. Every public print on this venue had a real resting maker on the other side, so the tape is the population of achievable maker fills at perfect queue priority. Measured there across 134 settled markets, clustered one observation per market: +1.01¢/contract, t = 1.55. At half that sample it read t = 1.98, so more data made the effect weaker, and the favourite/underdog split flipped sign outright. That is noise, not a small real edge.

Four times on this desk our own instrumentation produced a spectacular number that evaporated under scrutiny (+103.7%, +944%, t=+23.3, and a concurrency bug that was silently deleting filled positions). The P&L above is real bookkeeping on paper trades; it is not evidence of edge, and a green stretch never was — P(week > 0) exceeds 76% even under a no-edge null.
